:::paperq 1
Express
$$\frac{7x - 1}{(x-1)(2x+1)}$$
in partial fractions. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 1 is 4 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 2
A sector of a circle has radius 8 cm and angle 0.9 radians at the centre.

**(a)** Find the length of the arc of the sector. [[2]]

**(b)** Find the area of the segment cut off by the chord joining the ends of the arc, giving your
answer to 3 significant figures. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 2 is 5 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 3
**(a)** Find the binomial expansion of
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{4 - x}}$$
in ascending powers of $x$, up to and including the term in $x^2$, giving each coefficient in its
simplest form. [[4]]

**(b)** State the range of values of $x$ for which your expansion is valid. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 3 is 6 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 4
A curve has equation
$$x^3 + y^3 = 6xy$$

**(a)** Show that
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2y - x^2}{y^2 - 2x}$$
[[4]]

**(b)** The point $P(3, 3)$ lies on the curve. Find an equation of the **normal** to the curve at $P$,
giving your answer in its simplest form. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 4 is 7 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 5
**(a)** Find $\displaystyle\int x\ln x \,dx$. [[4]]

**(b)** Hence evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^{e} x\ln x\,dx$, giving your answer in terms of $e$. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 5 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 6
$$f(x) = x^3 + 3x - 5$$

**(a)** Show that the equation $f(x) = 0$ has a root $\alpha$ between $x = 1$ and $x = 2$. [[2]]

**(b)** Using $x_0 = 1$, apply the Newton–Raphson method **twice** to $f(x)$ to obtain an
approximation for $\alpha$, giving your answer to 4 decimal places. [[3]]

**(c)** Show that $\alpha = 1.154$ correct to 3 decimal places. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 6 is 7 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 7
$$f(\theta) = 5\cos\theta + 12\sin\theta$$

**(a)** Express $f(\theta)$ in the form $R\sin(\theta + \alpha)$, where $R > 0$ and
$0 < \alpha < \frac\pi2$. Give the exact value of $R$ and give $\alpha$ to 4 decimal places. [[3]]

**(b)** Hence solve, for $0 \leq \theta \leq 2\pi$,
$$5\cos\theta + 12\sin\theta = 6$$
giving your answers to 3 decimal places. [[4]]

**(c)** Write down the maximum value of
$$\frac{1}{f(\theta) + 15}$$
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 7 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 8
The points $A$, $B$ and $C$ have position vectors
$$\overrightarrow{OA} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}, \qquad
\overrightarrow{OB} = 5\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}, \qquad
\overrightarrow{OC} = 11\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j} - 9\mathbf{k}$$

**(a)** Find $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\left|\overrightarrow{AB}\right|$, giving the magnitude in
surd form. [[3]]

**(b)** Prove that $A$, $B$ and $C$ are collinear. [[3]]

**(c)** Find the ratio $AB : BC$. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 8 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 9
A cup of coffee cools in a room held at a constant $20\,^\circ$C. Its temperature $\theta\,^\circ$C
after $t$ minutes is modelled by the differential equation
$$\frac{d\theta}{dt} = -k(\theta - 20)$$
where $k$ is a positive constant. Initially the coffee is at $80\,^\circ$C.

**(a)** Solve the differential equation to show that
$$\theta = 20 + 60e^{-kt}$$
[[4]]

**(b)** Given that the coffee has cooled to $50\,^\circ$C after 10 minutes, find the exact value
of $k$. [[2]]

**(c)** Find the temperature of the coffee after 25 minutes, to 3 significant figures. [[2]]

**(d)** State what the model predicts about the temperature of the coffee in the long term, and
explain why this is reasonable. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 9 is 10 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 10
**(a)** Prove by contradiction that $\sqrt5$ is irrational. [[5]]

**(b)** Prove that $n^3 - n$ is divisible by 6 for all integers $n$. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 10 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 11
A curve has parametric equations
$$x = t^2 - 1, \qquad y = t^3 - 3t$$

**(a)** Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ in terms of $t$. [[3]]

**(b)** Find the coordinates of the two points on the curve at which the tangent is parallel to the
$x$-axis. [[4]]

**(c)** Find an equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where $t = 2$, giving your answer
in the form $ax + by + c = 0$. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 11 is 10 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 12
A sequence is defined by
$$u_{n+1} = 0.4u_n + 6, \qquad u_1 = 5$$

**(a)** Find the values of $u_2$, $u_3$ and $u_4$. [[3]]

**(b)** Given that the sequence converges, find the exact value of its limit. [[3]]

**(c)** Find $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{4} u_n$. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 12 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 13
The functions $f$ and $g$ are defined by
$$f(x) = \frac{3}{x - 2}, \; x > 2 \qquad\qquad g(x) = x^2 + 1, \; x \in \mathbb{R}$$

**(a)** Find $fg(x)$, and state the set of values of $x$ for which $fg(x)$ is defined. [[3]]

**(b)** Find $f^{-1}(x)$ and state its domain. [[4]]

**(c)** State the range of $f$. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 13 is 9 marks)*
:::
