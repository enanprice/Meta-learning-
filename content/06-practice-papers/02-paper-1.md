:::paperq 1
**(a)** Simplify fully $\sqrt{50} + \sqrt{18}$, giving your answer in the form $k\sqrt2$.
[[2]]

**(b)** Express $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt5 - 1}$ in the form $\dfrac{a\sqrt5 + b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$
are integers.
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 1 is 4 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 2
$$f(x) = 2x^2 - 12x + 23$$

**(a)** Express $f(x)$ in the form $a(x+b)^2 + c$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are constants to be found.
[[3]]

**(b)** Hence write down the minimum value of $f(x)$ and the value of $x$ at which it occurs.
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 2 is 5 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 3
**(a)** Find the first four terms, in ascending powers of $x$, of the binomial expansion of
$(1 + 3x)^8$, giving each term in its simplest form.
[[4]]

**(b)** Use your expansion, with a suitable value of $x$, to find an estimate for $1.03^8$, giving your
answer to 4 decimal places.
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 3 is 6 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 4
The circle $C$ has equation
$$x^2 + y^2 - 8x + 4y - 5 = 0$$

**(a)** Find the coordinates of the centre of $C$ and the radius of $C$.
[[3]]

**(b)** Verify that the point $P(8, 1)$ lies on $C$.
[[1]]

**(c)** Find an equation of the tangent to $C$ at $P$, giving your answer in the form
$ax + by + c = 0$ where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers.
[[3]]

*(Total for Question 4 is 7 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 5
A curve has equation
$$y = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x + 2$$

**(a)** Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$.
[[2]]

**(b)** Find the coordinates of the two stationary points of the curve.
[[3]]

**(c)** Determine the nature of each stationary point, showing your reasoning.
[[3]]

*(Total for Question 5 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 6
**(a)** Solve, for $x$,
$$3^{2x} - 10\left(3^{x}\right) + 9 = 0$$
[[4]]

**(b)** Solve, for $x > 0$,
$$\log_2(x+3) + \log_2 x = 2$$
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 6 is 6 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 7
**(a)** Show that the equation
$$3\sin^2 x = 4 - 4\cos x$$
can be written in the form
$$3\cos^2 x - 4\cos x + 1 = 0$$
[[3]]

**(b)** Hence solve, for $0° \leq x \leq 360°$,
$$3\sin^2 x = 4 - 4\cos x$$
giving your answers to 1 decimal place where appropriate.
[[6]]

*(Total for Question 7 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 8
The curve $C$ has equation $y = x^2 - 2x + 4$ and the line $l$ has equation $y = x + 2$.

**(a)** Find the coordinates of the two points where $l$ intersects $C$.
[[3]]

**(b)** Find the exact area of the finite region enclosed between $C$ and $l$.
[[5]]

*(Total for Question 8 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 9
An arithmetic sequence has 5th term 17 and 12th term 45.

**(a)** Find the first term and the common difference.
[[4]]

**(b)** Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the sequence.
[[3]]

*(Total for Question 9 is 7 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 10
The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = |2x - 5|$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

**(a)** Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$, stating the coordinates of the vertex and of the point where
the graph meets the $y$-axis.
[[3]]

**(b)** Solve the equation $f(x) = x + 1$.
[[4]]

**(c)** State the range of $f$.
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 10 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 11
An open-topped box is to be made with a square base of side $x$ cm and a volume of
$500\text{ cm}^3$.

**(a)** Show that the external surface area $A\text{ cm}^2$ of the box is given by
$$A = x^2 + \frac{2000}{x}$$
[[4]]

**(b)** Use calculus to find the value of $x$ for which $A$ is a minimum, justifying that your value
gives a minimum, and find this minimum surface area.
[[6]]

*(Total for Question 11 is 10 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 12
**(a)** Prove that the sum of the squares of any two consecutive odd numbers is even but is **not**
divisible by 4.
[[5]]

**(b)** Given that
$$f(x) = 2x^3 + x^2 - 13x + 6$$
show that $(x + 3)$ is a factor of $f(x)$, and hence factorise $f(x)$ completely.
[[5]]

*(Total for Question 12 is 10 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 13
The number of bacteria, $N$, in a culture after $t$ hours is modelled by
$$N = A e^{kt}$$
where $A$ and $k$ are constants.

Initially there are 300 bacteria. After 5 hours there are 1200 bacteria.

**(a)** Find the value of $A$ and show that $k = 0.277$ to 3 significant figures.
[[4]]

**(b)** Using the model, find the number of bacteria after 12 hours, giving your answer to
3 significant figures.
[[2]]

**(c)** Using the model, find the time taken for the number of bacteria to reach 10 000. Give your
answer in hours to 2 decimal places.
[[3]]

**(d)** State one limitation of this model.
[[2]]

*(Total for Question 13 is 11 marks)*
:::
