## Section A — Statistics

:::paperq 1
A company employs 800 people: 300 in production, 350 in sales and 150 in administration. A stratified
sample of 80 employees is to be taken.

**(a)** Find the number of employees that should be sampled from each department. [[3]]

**(b)** Give one advantage of using a stratified sample rather than a simple random sample in this
situation. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 1 is 5 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 2
For a sample of 40 observations of a variable $x$,
$$\sum x = 1240, \qquad \sum x^2 = 40\,600$$

**(a)** Find the mean of $x$. [[1]]

**(b)** Find the standard deviation of $x$, giving your answer to 3 significant figures. [[3]]

**(c)** The variable $y$ is defined by $y = \dfrac{x - 30}{5}$. Write down the mean and the standard
deviation of $y$. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 2 is 7 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 3
The table shows the time, in minutes, taken by 80 people to complete a task.

| Time $t$ (min) | $0 \leq t < 10$ | $10 \leq t < 15$ | $15 \leq t < 25$ | $25 \leq t < 40$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 15 | 20 | 30 | 15 |

**(a)** Calculate the frequency density for each class. [[2]]

**(b)** Use linear interpolation to estimate the median time. [[3]]

**(c)** Estimate the number of people who took longer than 20 minutes, stating an assumption you have
made. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 3 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 4
For two events $A$ and $B$,
$$P(A) = 0.4, \qquad P(B) = 0.35, \qquad P(A \cup B) = 0.6$$

**(a)** Find $P(A \cap B)$. [[2]]

**(b)** Find $P(A \mid B)$. [[2]]

**(c)** Determine, showing your reasoning, whether $A$ and $B$ are independent. [[2]]

**(d)** Find $P(A' \cap B')$. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 4 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 5
A manufacturer claims that 15% of the components it produces are faulty. A quality inspector takes a
random sample of 30 components and finds that 8 are faulty. The inspector believes the proportion of
faulty components has increased.

**(a)** Write down the distribution of the number of faulty components in the sample, assuming the
manufacturer's claim is correct. [[1]]

**(b)** Carry out a hypothesis test at the 5% significance level to test the inspector's belief.
State your hypotheses clearly. [[6]]

**(c)** Find the critical region for this test, and state its actual significance level. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 5 is 10 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 6
The mass, in grams, of an apple from a particular orchard is modelled by a normal distribution with
mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$.

It is known that 10% of apples have a mass less than 120 g, and 25% have a mass greater than 155 g.

**(a)** Find the value of $\mu$ and the value of $\sigma$, giving each to 3 significant figures. [[6]]

**(b)** Using your values, find the probability that a randomly chosen apple has a mass between
130 g and 160 g. [[3]]

**(c)** A random sample of 20 apples is taken. Find the probability that at least 3 of them have a
mass less than 120 g. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 6 is 12 marks)*
:::

## Section B — Mechanics

:::paperq 7
A car travelling along a straight horizontal road accelerates uniformly from $8\text{ m s}^{-1}$ to
$20\text{ m s}^{-1}$ in 6 seconds. It then decelerates uniformly to rest in a further 10 seconds.

**(a)** Find the acceleration of the car during the first stage. [[2]]

**(b)** Find the total distance travelled by the car. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 7 is 6 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 8
A car of mass 1500 kg tows a trailer of mass 500 kg along a straight horizontal road. The engine
provides a constant driving force of 4000 N. The resistance to motion is 900 N on the car and 300 N
on the trailer.

**(a)** Find the acceleration of the car and trailer. [[3]]

**(b)** Find the tension in the tow bar. [[3]]

**(c)** State one assumption you have made about the tow bar, and explain how it is used in your
working. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 8 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 9
A particle moves along a straight line. At time $t$ seconds its velocity is
$$v = 3t^2 - 12t + 9 \quad \text{m s}^{-1}, \qquad t \geq 0$$

**(a)** Find the times at which the particle is instantaneously at rest. [[3]]

**(b)** Find the acceleration of the particle when $t = 4$. [[2]]

**(c)** Find the total distance travelled by the particle in the first 4 seconds. [[3]]

*(Total for Question 9 is 8 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 10
A uniform beam $AB$ of length 6 m and mass 30 kg rests horizontally on two supports at $C$ and $D$,
where $AC = 1$ m and $AD = 4$ m. A block of mass 50 kg is placed on the beam at a distance $x$ m
from $A$.

**(a)** Given that $x = 2$, find the magnitude of the reaction at each support. [[5]]

**(b)** Find the greatest value of $x$ for which the beam does not tilt. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 10 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 11
A ball is projected from a point $O$ on horizontal ground with speed $21\text{ m s}^{-1}$ at an angle
of $35°$ above the horizontal. The ball is modelled as a particle moving freely under gravity.

**(a)** Find the time of flight of the ball. [[3]]

**(b)** Find the horizontal range of the ball. [[2]]

**(c)** Find the two horizontal distances from $O$ at which the ball is 4 m above the ground. [[4]]

*(Total for Question 11 is 9 marks)*
:::

:::paperq 12
A block $P$ of mass 5 kg lies on a rough plane inclined at $20°$ to the horizontal. The coefficient of
friction between $P$ and the plane is 0.3. $P$ is connected by a light inextensible string, which
passes over a smooth pulley fixed at the top of the plane, to a block $Q$ of mass 8 kg hanging freely.

The system is released from rest.

**(a)** Show that the system begins to move. [[3]]

**(b)** Find the acceleration of the system and the tension in the string. [[5]]

**(c)** State one assumption you have made about the string, and explain its effect on your
working. [[2]]

*(Total for Question 12 is 10 marks)*
:::
