Pure — Year 12 (AS)

The AS pure content: algebra, graphs, trigonometry, calculus and logs.

14 topics
  • P1.1Algebraic ExpressionsIndex laws, expanding, factorising, surds and rationalising denominators — the toolkit everything else is built from.
  • P1.2QuadraticsSolving, completing the square, the discriminant, sketching parabolas, and spotting hidden quadratics.
  • P1.3Equations and InequalitiesSimultaneous equations (including one quadratic), linear and quadratic inequalities, set notation, and shading regions.
  • P1.4Graphs and TransformationsCubics, quartics, reciprocal graphs, asymptotes, intersections, and the four graph transformations.
  • P1.5Straight Line GraphsGradients, all three forms of a line equation, parallel and perpendicular lines, distances, midpoints and modelling.
  • P1.6CirclesThe equation of a circle, completing the square to find centre and radius, tangents, chords, and the circle theorems that get examined.
  • P1.7Algebraic Methods and ProofSimplifying algebraic fractions, polynomial division, the factor theorem, and the four styles of proof.
  • P1.8The Binomial ExpansionPascal's triangle, the nCr formula, expanding $(a+bx)^n$, finding single terms, and using expansions for approximations.
  • P1.9Trigonometric RatiosExact values, the sine and cosine rules, the ambiguous case, the area formula, and trig in 3D and bearings.
  • P1.10Trigonometric Identities and EquationsThe trig graphs, the CAST diagram, the two Year 12 identities, and a reliable procedure for solving trig equations in an interval.
  • P1.11Vectors (2D)Vector notation, magnitude and direction, adding and scaling, position vectors, unit vectors, and geometric proofs.
  • P1.12DifferentiationGradient functions from first principles, the power rule, tangents and normals, stationary points and their nature, and optimisation.
  • P1.13IntegrationIntegration as the reverse of differentiation, the constant of integration, definite integrals, and areas under and between curves.
  • P1.14Exponentials and LogarithmsExponential graphs, the number e, logarithm laws, solving exponential equations, and turning curved data into straight lines.

Pure — Year 13 (A2)

The A2 pure content: functions, series, advanced trig, harder calculus and 3D vectors.

12 topics
  • P2.1Proof by Contradiction and Partial FractionsProof by contradiction, the irrationality of √2, and splitting algebraic fractions into simpler pieces.
  • P2.2Functions, Modulus and Graph TransformationsDomain and range, composite and inverse functions, the modulus function, and solving modulus equations and inequalities.
  • P2.3Sequences and SeriesArithmetic and geometric sequences, sum formulae, convergence, sigma notation, recurrence relations and periodic sequences.
  • P2.4Binomial Expansion for Negative and Fractional IndicesExtending the binomial expansion to any index, the validity condition, expanding $(a+bx)^n$, and combining with partial fractions.
  • P2.5RadiansWhy radians exist, converting, arc length and sector area, exact values, and the small angle approximations.
  • P2.6Reciprocal and Inverse Trigonometric Functionssec, cosec and cot, their graphs, the two Pythagorean identities, and the inverse functions arcsin, arccos and arctan.
  • P2.7Addition Formulae, Double Angles and the R FormThe compound angle formulae, double angle identities, and writing $a\cos\theta + b\sin\theta$ as a single wave.
  • P2.8Parametric EquationsDescribing curves with a parameter, converting to Cartesian form, trig parameters, and parametric differentiation.
  • P2.9Advanced DifferentiationDerivatives of trig, exponential and log functions; chain, product and quotient rules; implicit differentiation; connected rates of change; concavity and inflection.
  • P2.10Numerical MethodsLocating roots by sign change, fixed-point iteration, staircase and cobweb diagrams, and the Newton–Raphson method.
  • P2.11Advanced Integration and Differential EquationsStandard integrals, reverse chain rule, substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, the trapezium rule and separable differential equations.
  • P2.12Vectors in Three DimensionsExtending vectors to 3D, magnitude and direction cosines, distance, and solving geometric problems in space.
